Spaces & Formatting

EntityWidthDescription
&nbsp;Normal spaceNon-breaking space (prevents line break)
&ensp;En spaceHalf an em (width of 'n')
&emsp;Em spaceFull em (width of 'm')
&thinsp;Thin space1/5 or 1/6 of an em
&#8203;Zero widthZero-width space (allows line break)
&#8204;Zero widthZero-width non-joiner
&shy;Zero widthSoft hyphen (break point for long words)
&#10;—Line feed (in attributes)
Tip: Use &nbsp; to prevent two words from being split across lines. Use &shy; inside long words to hint where they can be hyphenated if needed.
